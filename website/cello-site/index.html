Overview

A non-invasive route to bladder cancer diagnosis

Bladder cancer has one of the highest recurrence rates of any solid tumour, which means patients face years of repeated surveillance. The clinical standard, cystoscopy, is invasive and uncomfortable; urine cytology is non-invasive but frequently misses low-grade tumours.

CELLo investigates whether cells shed naturally into urine can be enriched, imaged and classified automatically, closely enough to clinical-grade accuracy to complement or reduce the need for cystoscopy. The project spans microfluidic cell enrichment, multimodal imaging (brightfield, phase-contrast, quantitative phase imaging), and deep learning models — including vision transformers and biomedical vision-language models — trained to detect, segment and stage urothelial cells.

Work is carried out jointly by INESC TEC (imaging, computer vision and machine learning) and the Faculty of Medicine of the University of Porto (cell biology, biophysics and clinical validation), with samples collected at Centro Hospitalar Universitário de São João (CHUSJ), Porto.

  • 01Urine samples are collected from healthy controls, bladder cancer patients and patients in post-treatment follow-up.
  • 02Cell enrichment on a microfluidic chip concentrates the most diagnostically relevant cells.
  • 03Cell imaging captures each cell under several complementary microscopy modalities.
  • 04AI analysis detects, segments and classifies cells by tumour stage and grade.
“To improve non-invasive bladder cancer diagnosis and follow-up using urine-cell analysis.” — CELLo project goal

Conference paper · IEEE EMBC 2026

Enhancing Cytological Staining-Free Image Classification with Robust Vision Transformers

IEEE Engineering in Medicine & Biology Society (EMBC) 2026 — Hugo S. Oliveira, Pedro C. Carvalho, Raquel L. Monteiro, Daniela Ferreira-Santos, Tânia Pereira, Raphaël F. Canadas, Hélder P. Oliveira

Proposes a DINO-pretrained, DeiT-III-based vision transformer with a SERF attention activation and an asymmetric loss for class imbalance, applied to staining-free bladder cancer cell classification from the CELLo dataset. Benchmarked against ViT, ConvNeXtV2 and ResNet-50 across CELLo, ISIC 2019 and a bladder-tissue endoscopy dataset.

Journal article · Research article

Leveraging Pretrained Neural Network Models for the Classification of Tumor Cells Analyzed by Label-Free Phase Holotomographic Microscopy

Computational and Structural Biotechnology Journal (CSBJ) · Published 9 June 2026 — Leonor V. C. Losa, Temple A. Douglas, Lia Santos, Raquel Monteiro, Isabel Calejo, Raphaël F. Canadas, Jana B. Nieder

Uses 3D phase holotomographic microscopy with pretrained CNNs (VGG16, ResNet18, DenseNet121, EfficientNet-B0) to classify treatment status in lung cancer cells label-free, reaching 96.9% accuracy, then extends the same pipeline to separate high- from low-grade urothelial cancer cells with 90.6% accuracy.

MSc thesis · Faculty of Sciences, University of Porto (FCUP)

Progressive Fine-Tuning of Vision-Language Models for Urine Cytology-Based Bladder Cancer Staging

Master in Bioinformatics and Computational Biology — Pedro José Costa Oliveira, 2026

Adapts BiomedCLIP, a pretrained biomedical vision-language model, to bladder cancer staging from urine cytology through a progressive fine-tuning strategy (linear probing to full unfreezing), reporting a balanced accuracy of 94.04% across five tumour stages and generalisation to external imaging datasets.

Conference paper · VISAPP 2025

Robust Cell Segmentation in Urine Cytology Images for Bladder Cancer Diagnosis

International Conference on Computer Vision Theory and Applications (VISAPP) 2025 — Mariana L. Teixeira, Hugo S. Oliveira, Raquel L. Monteiro, Daniela Ferreira, Tânia Pereira, Raphaël F. Canadas, Hélder P. Oliveira

Benchmarks classical (Otsu, thresholding, hybrid pipelines, Cellpose) against deep-learning segmentation (DeepCell, a CNN enhanced with self-supervised DINO ViT features) on the CELLo brightfield dataset, finding hybrid methods handle noise and overlaps well, while DeepCell achieves the highest accuracy and detection rate.

Technology transfer · Patent pending · TRL 3

BCPANEL — Multiparametric Biophysical Profiling for Bladder Cancer Diagnosis and Stratification

U.Porto Inovação, Faculty of Medicine, University of Porto · Process no. PT121776 — Inventors: Raphaël Canadas, Isabel Calejo

A multiparametric biophysical profiling approach analysing morphological, optical, clustering, dry-mass and zeta-potential properties of single cells, combined through machine learning to identify bladder-cancer-associated phenotypes and support stratification by grade, stage, muscle invasiveness and EMT phenotype from urine samples.
